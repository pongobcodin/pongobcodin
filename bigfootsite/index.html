What is Electrolysis?

Electrolysis is a specialized process where a tiny, fine probe is inserted into the natural opening of the hair follicle. A small amount of electrical energy is then applied to destroy the hair growth tissue.

Unlike other methods that simply stun the hair, electrolysis ensures the follicle can never produce a hair again. It is truly permanent.

Modalities: Thermolysis, Blend, and Galvanic

Thermolysis

Thermolysis uses a short burst of radiofrequency to heat and destroy the follicle. Clients commonly feel a quick, sharp pinch; recovery is fast and it's effective for fine or shorter hairs.

Galvanic

Galvanic electrolysis uses a direct current to create a chemical reaction that dissolves the hair root. Treatments are gentler but take longer per hair, and are often chosen for coarser or stubborn follicles.

Blend

The blend method combines thermolysis and galvanic currents to apply heat and chemical action together, increasing effectiveness on resistant follicles. It can feel slightly more intense but can reduce total sessions for difficult areas.

What to expect: During your consultation we’ll recommend the best modality for your hair type and comfort. Session length and frequency depend on the area treated; settings are adjusted to balance effectiveness and tolerance.
